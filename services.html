Spa Etiquette & Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful advice to ensure your sanctuary experience is seamless and peaceful.

When should I arrive for my appointment?

+
We kindly recommend arriving 15 to 20 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment. This allows you to check in without haste, change into our comfortable robes, sip a welcome herbal tea, and complete your personalized wellness consultation.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

+
Because private suites and certified therapists are reserved exclusively for your session, we request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 12 hours' notice may be subject to a 50% reservation fee.

What should I wear during my treatments?

+
You may disrobe to your comfort level. For massage and body treatments, single-use spa undergarments are provided. During all sessions, our therapists use professional draping techniques to ensure your privacy is respected at all times.

Are treatments suitable during pregnancy or for medical conditions?

+
Yes, we offer specialized prenatal massage therapies for guests past their first trimester (12 weeks). Please notify our concierge of any health conditions, allergies, or injuries when booking so we can tailor the safest, most restorative experience.
